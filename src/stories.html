How to write a story

A story is a list of scenes. Each scene has lines, then either choices or a jump to another scene.
Characters: @show Annalie left puts her on screen (left, center, right, or a number: -1 is left, 0 the middle, 1 right, so @show Annalie -0.5 is halfway to the left), @move Annalie 0.3 slides her to a new spot without showing her again, @move Annalie 0.3 -0.5 (the last number is height, negative is lower), @slide Melissa -1.6 9 glides her there over 9 seconds (the story keeps going while she moves) and @stop Melissa freezes her where she is, @size Annalie 0.8 makes her smaller or bigger (1 = the game's size; you can also put it last on a show line, @show Annalie 0.1 0.8), @hide Annalie or @hide all removes her. Faces: write them as tags on her lines, Annalie [sad]: ..., or change them with @face Annalie pervygrin. Annalie's faces are normal, eyeclosed (happy), sad, shocked, pervygrin. Outfits: @outfit Annalie Halloween_Outfit or @outfit Annalie default. Backgrounds: @background clothier fades to black, switches to that place, and fades back (room, outside, shop, clothier, shower, pc, cuddle, changingroom, hospital, flashback). @jun -1.1 1.4 sets her size too (the second number, 1 = her room size), @jun left puts Jun on screen in the outside, shop and clothier backgrounds (left, center, right, a number like @jun -1.4, or hide); in the room she is always there. When the story ends it returns to where you were. Music: @music Rainy Day plays one of the game's own tracks (the name is matched loosely against the game's track names, e.g. Fishing_Rainy_Day, Cuddle_If_Only, BadEnd_A_Soft_Decay), @music Cuddle plays a whole category and the game shuffles it, @music stop gives the game's own music back, @music auto goes back to the default for the background. Without any @music, each @background picks a fitting track by itself (outside: street music, shop: Cashier theme, clothier: Annalie theme, hospital and flashback: sad story tracks). The music is put back when the story ends. The full list of track names is written to the log ({{LOG}}) the first time a story plays music. Her lines are Annalie: text. These are a first version: press F7 in the game for the full list of IDs.
Name colours: the tag colour comes from the game (Annalie purple, Melissa green, the player is called You, and so on). For your own characters or to override one, add color: Name = #rrggbb near the top.

// notes start with //

# start
Narrator: Rain taps on the window.
{bot} [happy][wave]: Hello, {player}!
{player}: Hi.
? Ask about her day -> day
? Just sit with her -> sit

# day
{bot} [shy]: It was fine.
-> end

# sit
{bot} [veryhappy]: Thank you.
-> end

# name starts a scene; the first one is the start.

Name [tags]: text a spoken line. Use {bot} and {player} for the names. Narrator shows no name.

? text -> scene a choice button. -> scene jumps, -> end finishes the story.

Tags on {bot} lines change her face and gestures: happy, sad, angry, shy, surprised (add very), wave, point, lecture, clench.

In the game press F3 to pick a story, and F3 again to stop one. Her mouth moves and the camera zooms in while it plays.

Stories are plain files in {{ROOT}}\jun_stories.
